# Changelog

What changed in each version of Vanguard Item Manager. The app shows this file in its "What's new" window and on the `/changelog` page, so keep its format: a `## version - YYYY-MM-DD` heading per release (newest first), then `### New`, `### Improved` and `### Fixed` sections with one `-` item per change. The French translation is in [CHANGELOG.fr.md](CHANGELOG.fr.md).

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-04

### New

- Armor Optimizer: set a minimum and a priority for each stat, pick an exotic and up to two set bonuses, and equip the best combination with its +10 stat mods in one click.
- Inventory Cleaner: send a character's unequipped gear to the vault, or list the junk to dismantle (worse duplicates, low stat armor, low tier gear).
- Gear and mod editor: change the weapons, armor and mods of a character from the character view.
- Subclass editor: change the super, abilities, aspects and fragments from the character view, with the stats updated as you go.
- The interface is translated in French and follows the language chosen in the settings.
- Right-click an item to move it to a character or to the vault, or to edit its mods.
- Sort the vault by name, power, rarity, tier, type or newest.
- A "What's new" window after each update, and the full changelog in the settings.

### Improved

- Loadouts: swap two loadouts between their slots, and save the artifact and cosmetics with them.
- New item tooltip in the game's style: every perk column, selectable perks, the applied ornament, and it follows the item.
- Reworked settings: automatic refresh, tooltip speed, animations, notification duration and vault item size.
- Logging out asks for confirmation first.

### Fixed

- Tall tooltips no longer go past the bottom of the screen.
- Aligned the loadout grid and the spacing of the editors.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-07

### New

- Every item of your characters, vault and postmaster in one view.
- Search by name, perk, slot, element, tier, power or stat, in the search window and in the vault.
- Create, edit and equip loadouts.
- Equip, move, pull from the postmaster, lock and unlock items.
